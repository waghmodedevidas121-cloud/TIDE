Key decisions

  • Integer simulation units, 60 per cell at a fixed 60 Hz tick. Deep = 4 units/tick (4 cells/s), shallow = 2 units/tick (2 cells/s). Integer maths is deterministic; floats are not.
  • Turns consumed only on a cell line**, and every step is clamped so it never overshoots that line. A slow cell can stall a turn by at most one tick. This is the rule that makes Paper.io feel crisp, and it is why diagonal smoothness is out of the question.
  • Sand and border block instead of killing. Phase 1 scope discipline: only one new death cause at a time, and adding a fifth terrain-death rule would muddy the M4 attribution test. Blocked boat flashes red.
  • Buffer before handing off. WASD/arrows and swipe only set pendingHeading; the core decides when to apply it. Input cannot skip a cell.
